About Us & Help

What is
Andik's Desserts?

Andik's Desserts Mini Cafe system is a digital menu and inventory viewer for customers — so you always know what's available, what's in stock, and what's new. No orders, no account needed. Just browse and enjoy.

How to Use This System

Step 1
Browse the Menu
See all available desserts with photos, prices, and categories.
Step 2
Check Stock
Stock badges show whether an item is available, low, or sold out.
Step 3
Read Reviews
See star ratings and customer reviews on each menu item page.
Step 4
Stay Updated
Check the Announcements page for the latest news from the cafe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I place an order through this website?
No — this system is for browsing only. To order, please visit the cafe in person or contact the staff directly via WhatsApp.
The stock says available but the item was sold out. Why?
Stock is updated by staff manually. There may be a short delay between a real-world change and what's shown here. Please contact the staff to confirm before heading over.
I see wrong information on a menu item. What do I do?
Please reach out to the staff via WhatsApp or email (details below). Let them know the item name and what looks incorrect — they'll get it fixed.
Do I need to create an account to browse?
No account is needed to view the menu, check stock, or read announcements. Accounts are for staff and admin only.
Where is Andik's Desserts located?
We're located at Kolej 9, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, Johor Bahru. Drop by anytime during operating hours!
